制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

ログ候補は `frameMs/passId/passMs/drawCalls/triangleCount/pipelineCacheHit/workingColorSpace/toneMappingMode/splatLod/coarseFirstRank/rangeStart/rangeEnd/webglFallbackLoaded/xrEyeIndex/xrViewport/xrHitDistance/controllerSqueeze`。64x8のstate textureでは横を直近フレーム、縦をscene/asset/pass/XR inputに割り当てる。短期は毎フレームの数値をshard密度とbeam幅へ直接マップする。中長期では、fallbackやrange到着も含め、編集可能なcut scoreとして記録・再生する。

Useful fields are `frameMs/passId/passMs/drawCalls/triangleCount/pipelineCacheHit/workingColorSpace/toneMappingMode/splatLod/coarseFirstRank/rangeStart/rangeEnd/webglFallbackLoaded/xrEyeIndex/xrViewport/xrHitDistance/controllerSqueeze`. In a 64x8 state texture, assign recent frames horizontally and scene/asset/pass/XR input vertically. Short-term, map each frame directly to shard density and beam width; long-term, record and replay fallback and range arrivals as an editable cut score.

サンプルデータの可能性と組み合わせ考察Possibilities for the sample data and combinations

この2本の入力 `focusX/focusY/mode/pulse/frameMs/passMs/drawCalls/splatLod/xrEyeIndex/xrHitDistance/controllerSqueeze` は、作品の編集ログでもある。音ならkickをcut pulse、bassをpressure密度、highをshatterに割り当てられる。カメラ/MediaPipeなら顔の向きでgaze、手の握りでcontroller impulseを作れる。WebXRでは視線に近いsplatだけ詳細化し、負荷が上がるほど遷移粒子が増える展示にできる。ブランド演出ならアクセス集中や閲覧導線を匿名のtime scoreにして、サイト自身の利用状況がWebfilmを少しずつ編集する形へ発展できる。

Inputs such as `focusX/focusY/mode/pulse/frameMs/passMs/drawCalls/splatLod/xrEyeIndex/xrHitDistance/controllerSqueeze` are also an editing log. Audio can map kick to cut pulse, bass to pressure density, and highs to shatter. Camera/MediaPipe can turn face direction into gaze and grip into controller impulse. In WebXR, nearby splats refine while load increases transition particles. For brands, anonymous traffic and browsing flow can become a time score that gradually edits the webfilm itself.
