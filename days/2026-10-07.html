注目事例と何が面白いかNotable examples and why they matter

今日面白いのは、HTMLやInspectorのような『UI寄りの面』が、単なるデバッグ画面ではなくshader素材として扱えること。three.js HTMLTextureはHTML-in-Canvas API変化への追従が続いており、DOM的なdirty rectやテキスト面をGPU上の質感として扱いやすい。PlayCanvas particle fog/tonemapは、粒子をシーンのfogやexposureから切り離す/接続するスイッチとして演出に使える。Babylon LiteのInspector LiteやGPU textは、軽量WebGPUアプリの観測面そのものを作品内レイヤーにしやすい。Codropsのcursor trailは、ポインタの遅れや形状の履歴をTSL/WebGPU表現に戻す好例で、10/6のtemporal historyと接続しやすい。

The interesting thread today is that UI-like surfaces such as HTML and inspectors can become shader material rather than just debug screens. three.js HTMLTexture work follows HTML-in-Canvas API changes, making DOM-like dirty rects and text surfaces easier to treat as GPU texture material. PlayCanvas particle fog/tonemap controls can become an expressive switch that connects or disconnects particles from scene fog and exposure. Babylon Lite's Inspector Lite and GPU text make the observation surface of a lightweight WebGPU app feel like an in-scene layer. Codrops cursor-trail work is a strong reference for turning pointer lag and shape history back into TSL/WebGPU expression, connecting naturally with 10/6's temporal history.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

ログ化候補は `htmlCanvasDirtyRect/htmlTextureCommitMs/domSurfaceId/chromiumHtmlCanvasApiVersion/nodeBuildCacheKey/materialStructureHash/bufferDisposed/particleUseFog/particleUseTonemap/audioAssetLoaded/gsplatLodMode/pointerPixelRatio/inspectorLayerId/gpuTextGlyphCount/splatReadbackRows/deviceLostRecovered/offscreenWorkerFrame`。UIやInspectorをそのまま重ねるより、dirty rect、glyph count、readback row、particle fog係数を低解像度のstate textureに詰め、fragment shader側で線・粒・残像へ変換すると、debug感を保ちつつ作品化できる。

Candidate log fields include `htmlCanvasDirtyRect/htmlTextureCommitMs/domSurfaceId/chromiumHtmlCanvasApiVersion/nodeBuildCacheKey/materialStructureHash/bufferDisposed/particleUseFog/particleUseTonemap/audioAssetLoaded/gsplatLodMode/pointerPixelRatio/inspectorLayerId/gpuTextGlyphCount/splatReadbackRows/deviceLostRecovered/offscreenWorkerFrame`. Instead of overlaying raw UI or inspector panels, pack dirty rects, glyph counts, readback rows, and particle fog factors into a low-resolution state texture, then let a fragment shader convert them into lines, particles, and trails. This keeps the diagnostic feeling while making it presentable.

The recent trend is that not only GPU pipeline internals but also surrounding surfaces such as HTML, inspectors, debug panels, and cursor trails are becoming expressive material. サンプルデータの可能性と組み合わせ考察Possibilities for the sample data and combinations

今回の2本は、入力を `probeX/probeY/mode/pulse/dirtyProbe/trailAttractor`、状態を `dirtyRectCount/commitMs/nodeBuildShared/cacheKey/materialHash/particleFog/particleTonemap/inspectorLayer/glyphCount/splatRow/readbackLatency` として保存できる。音反応ではkickでcommit pulse、snareでInspector layer切替、hi-hatでdirty grid scan、bassでparticle fogを動かす。カメラやMediaPipeを足すなら、手の速度をcursor trailの遅れへ、顔の距離をsplat readback範囲へ流す。WebXRではUI面を空間看板化し、controller rayが当たった場所だけHTML dirty surfaceが発光する構成がよい。

The two samples can store inputs as `probeX/probeY/mode/pulse/dirtyProbe/trailAttractor` and state as `dirtyRectCount/commitMs/nodeBuildShared/cacheKey/materialHash/particleFog/particleTonemap/inspectorLayer/glyphCount/splatRow/readbackLatency`. For audio, kick can trigger commit pulses, snare can switch inspector layers, hi-hats can scan the dirty grid, and bass can drive particle fog. With camera or MediaPipe, hand velocity can feed cursor-trail lag and face distance can control splat readback range. In WebXR, UI surfaces can become spatial signage, with only controller-ray hits lighting up the HTML dirty surface.
