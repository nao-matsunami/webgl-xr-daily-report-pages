The recent trend is that GPU safety fixes and engine maintenance items are becoming expressive vocabulary. September moved through readback, resource lifetime, sort precision, mipmap/residency, layer/depth; early October added viewport/scissor, bind groups, Inspector/AppStats, variants, HTML/cursor, depth/history, and splat shadows. Today adds coarse-first LOD and color-space contracts. A strong combination is mapping coarse LOD arrival to bass, tone mapping to mids, and texture update ranges to high-frequency noise. The high-value theme is exposing the unfinished states of loading, rebuilding, and color conversion as the artwork's breathing instead of hiding them.
