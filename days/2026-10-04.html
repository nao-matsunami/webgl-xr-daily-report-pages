注目事例と何が面白いかNotable examples and why they matter

今日の注目は、PlayCanvasのInspector / AppStats / TextureRendererとBabylonのframe graph reducerを同じ地図に置く発想。Inspectorは状態を列挙する道具だが、TextureRendererやdepth previewと合わせると、状態の位置や読み戻しの遅れそのものが空間演出になる。Babylonのmin/max reducerは、画面の値域をただ圧縮するだけでなく、どの領域が表現上の主語になっているかを示す針にできる。three.js r187のviewport/scissorやXR cameraの変更は、左右eyeやrender target単位の差分を見せる良い接続点。

The interesting move today is placing PlayCanvas Inspector/AppStats/TextureRenderer ideas and Babylon's frame-graph reducer on one map. Inspector is normally an enumeration tool, but combined with TextureRenderer and depth previews, state location and readback delay become spatial staging. Babylon's min/max reducer is not just compression of value ranges; it can become a needle showing which region is the expressive subject. three.js r187 viewport/scissor and XR-camera changes are good connection points for showing per-eye and per-render-target differences.

表現・インタラクションのアイデアExpression and interaction ideas

短期: depth tile、min/max reducer、readback queueを4象限のatlasにし、dragでprobe rectangle、clickでreadback pulseを走らせる。派生: XRの左右eye budget、residency priority、probe echoを重ね、controllerや視線の動きがGPU resourceの滞在時間を決めるように見せる。中長期: 9/29のresidency、10/1のviewport/scissor、10/2のbind group、10/3のInspector / AppStats、10/4のreadback delayを、1つのrender-state atlasとして統合する。

Short term: make depth tiles, min/max reducers, and readback queues into a four-quadrant atlas where dragging moves the probe rectangle and clicking emits a readback pulse. Derived idea: layer XR left/right eye budgets, residency priority, and probe echoes so controller or gaze motion appears to decide GPU-resource lifetime. Longer term: merge 9/29 residency, 10/1 viewport/scissor, 10/2 bind groups, 10/3 Inspector/AppStats, and 10/4 readback delay into one render-state atlas.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

ログ化するならPlayCanvas側は `frameId/viewId/passId/renderTargetId/depthTileId/readRectX/readRectY/readLatencyMs/resourceBuffers/resourceTextures/splatNodeDistance/splatResident`、Babylon側は `frameGraphBlock/minValue/maxValue/previousWorldMatrixValid/velocityMagnitude/textureRetryCount/mipmapLevel/effectError`、three.js側は `eyeIndex/xrCameraMatrixSource/scissorRect/viewportRect/renderTargetBound/pixelSize/pmremFace/finalizedAtFrame` を候補にする。shaderには最初から全部を入れず、まずは低解像度のstate textureへ量子化して、色、線幅、遅延軌跡に変換すると軽い。

For logging, candidate PlayCanvas fields are `frameId/viewId/passId/renderTargetId/depthTileId/readRectX/readRectY/readLatencyMs/resourceBuffers/resourceTextures/splatNodeDistance/splatResident`; Babylon fields are `frameGraphBlock/minValue/maxValue/previousWorldMatrixValid/velocityMagnitude/textureRetryCount/mipmapLevel/effectError`; three.js fields are `eyeIndex/xrCameraMatrixSource/scissorRect/viewportRect/renderTargetBound/pixelSize/pmremFace/finalizedAtFrame`. Do not put everything into the shader immediately; quantize first into a low-resolution state texture, then convert to color, line width, and delayed traces.

俯瞰メモBird's-eye memo

最近の傾向は、GPUの最終絵よりも、値がどの単位で渡され、どの順に読み戻され、どこで遅れ、どこで回復するかを作品化する方向に固まっている。9/29のresidency、10/1のviewport/scissor、10/2のbind group、10/3のInspector / AppStats、今日のdepth / reducer / readbackは、全部「見える内部状態」を観客が触れる地図にする試み。育てる価値が高いテーマは、debug HUDではなく、入力・音・XR視線で内部状態の読み取り方が変わるrenderer-state theater。

The recent direction is solidifying around making art from how values are submitted, read back, delayed, and recovered, rather than only from the final GPU image. 9/29 residency, 10/1 viewport/scissor, 10/2 bind groups, 10/3 Inspector/AppStats, and today's depth/reducer/readback work all aim to make visible internal state into a tactile map. The theme worth growing is renderer-state theater, not a debug HUD: input, audio, and XR gaze change how the internal state is read.

サンプルデータの可能性と組み合わせ考察Possibilities for the sample data and combinations

今回の2本は、入力を `probeX/probeY/gazeX/gazeY/pulse/mode`、描画状態を `readLatencyMs/depthTileId/minValue/maxValue/velocityMagnitude/eyeIndex/viewportRect/splatResident/resourceAge/recoveryPulse` として保存できる。音反応ではkickでread pulse、snareでrecovery pulse、低域でresidency ringを太らせる。WebXRでは左右eyeごとにreducer lanesを分け、controller velocityをprobe echoへ書く。MediaPipeでは手や顔の近いlandmarkからdepth tileが解像され、遠いlandmarkは遅延軌跡として残る構成が合う。

These two samples can store inputs as `probeX/probeY/gazeX/gazeY/pulse/mode` and render state as `readLatencyMs/depthTileId/minValue/maxValue/velocityMagnitude/eyeIndex/viewportRect/splatResident/resourceAge/recoveryPulse`. For audio, kick can trigger read pulses, snare can trigger recovery pulses, and bass can widen residency rings. For WebXR, split reducer lanes per eye and write controller velocity into the probe echo. With MediaPipe, nearby hand or face landmarks can resolve depth tiles first while farther landmarks remain as delayed traces.
