注目事例と何が面白いかNotable examples and why they matter

three.js #34597は、単なるbug watchではなく、fallback pathで何が作られ、何が破棄され、何が残るかを可視化する設計課題として面白い。WebGPUがないiOSなどではWebGPURendererがWebGL2 backendで動くため、fallbackは保険ではなく実ユーザーの描画経路になる。Codropsのlit GPU tubesは、線に厚み・法線・照明を持たせることで、単なる軌跡を空間内の物体に変える。これをresource lifetimeへつなげると、program / shader / VAO / bufferの寿命がチューブの太さ、明るさ、ほつれとして読める。

three.js #34597 matters not just as a bug watch item, but as a design prompt for visualizing what gets created, what gets deleted, and what remains on a fallback path. On WebGPU-less targets such as some iOS environments, WebGPURenderer's WebGL2 backend is not just insurance but the real rendering route. Codrops' lit GPU tubes show how thickness, normals, and lighting turn simple traces into spatial objects. Connected to resource lifetime, program / shader / VAO / buffer lifetimes can become tube thickness, brightness, and fraying.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

fallback診断の最小schemaは `cycleId`, `createdPrograms`, `deletedPrograms`, `createdShaders`, `deletedShaders`, `createdVAOs`, `deletedVAOs`, `deletedBuffers`, `rendererDisposed`, `backendName`, `forceWebGL`, `deviceLost`, `fallbackReason`。splat / alpha / root motionとつなぐ場合は、PlayCanvas側の `sortFlicker`, `sortKeyPrecision`, Babylon側の `alphaCutOff`, `rootMotionDelta`, `textureWaitMs` を同じframeに乗せる。重要なのは、値をただ数えるだけでなく、差分が溜まる場所を画面上の「古くなる素材」として扱うこと。

A minimal fallback-diagnostic schema is `cycleId`, `createdPrograms`, `deletedPrograms`, `createdShaders`, `deletedShaders`, `createdVAOs`, `deletedVAOs`, `deletedBuffers`, `rendererDisposed`, `backendName`, `forceWebGL`, `deviceLost`, and `fallbackReason`. To connect it with splat / alpha / root motion work, add PlayCanvas `sortFlicker` / `sortKeyPrecision` and Babylon `alphaCutOff` / `rootMotionDelta` / `textureWaitMs` on the same frame. The important move is not only counting values, but treating accumulated deltas as material that visibly ages on screen.
