サンプルデータの可能性と組み合わせ考察Possibilities for sample data and combinations

Per-eye retained draws, gaze, probe energy, sweep time and afterimage intensity form an audience-viewing score. Map audio, face pose, XR gaze and camera luminance to it for installations and material-led brand staging.
