注目事例と何が面白いかNotable examples and why they matter

Paper Crumple (VAT) が面白いのは、物理シミュレーションの結果を毎フレーム計算するのではなく、時間方向に焼いた変形や衝突記憶として見せられる点。これは9/18のsplat stream workerや9/19のuniform query visibilityと同じく、「完成画の裏にある時間状態」を表現へ戻す素材になる。Babylon.js側のnormalized occlusion queryは、見える/見えないを0/1ではなく連続値にし、VATの折れ、影の濃さ、音量、粒子密度へ直接つなげられる。frame graphにsplat / sprite / particle geometry passが入る流れは、紙片・粒子・影・音が同じpass graph上で遷移する演出に向いている。

Paper Crumple (VAT) is compelling because deformation and collision can be shown as baked temporal memory rather than recalculated physics every frame. Like September 18's splat stream worker and September 19's uniform query visibility work, it returns hidden time state behind the final image to the expressive surface. Babylon.js normalized occlusion queries make visibility continuous rather than binary, which can directly drive VAT folds, shadow density, audio volume, or particle density. The move to include splats / sprites / particles in frame-graph geometry rendering suits staging where paper fragments, particles, shadows, and sound evolve on one pass graph.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

VAT系は `vertexId`, `vatFrame`, `foldEnergy`, `collisionImpulse`, `shadowAge`, `creaseNormal`, `paperRestUv` を小さなtexture laneに落とすと、Three.jsのInstancedMesh / DataTexture / shaderMaterialへ移しやすい。occlusion系は `visibleSamples`, `totalSamples`, `visibilityWeight`, `queryAge`, `passId`, `resourceId` を連続値として保持し、音反応では低域=shadow memory、中域=fold impulse、高域=visibility sparkleへ割り当てると読みやすい。frame graph系は `passName`, `dependencyDepth`, `geometryKind`, `imageBitmapFastPath`, `compactGeneration`, `rebuildPulse` を持つと、Babylonのframe graphとPlayCanvas / SuperSplatのstream状態を同じschemaに寄せられる。

For VAT work, map `vertexId`, `vatFrame`, `foldEnergy`, `collisionImpulse`, `shadowAge`, `creaseNormal`, and `paperRestUv` into small texture lanes so they can move easily into Three.js InstancedMesh / DataTexture / shaderMaterial work. For occlusion, keep `visibleSamples`, `totalSamples`, `visibilityWeight`, `queryAge`, `passId`, and `resourceId` as continuous values. For audio response, low band can control shadow memory, mid band fold impulse, and high band visibility sparkle. For frame graphs, `passName`, `dependencyDepth`, `geometryKind`, `imageBitmapFastPath`, `compactGeneration`, and `rebuildPulse` can bridge Babylon frame graph state with PlayCanvas / SuperSplat streaming state.
