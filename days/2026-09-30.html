注目事例と何が面白いかNotable examples and why they matter

PlayCanvasのmaterialなしmesh受付修正は地味だが、実制作では「まだ素材が届いていないが配置だけはある」状態を捨てずに扱えることを意味する。v2.22.5のgsplat depth picking hang回避も、単に安定化ではなく、splatを編集・選択・測定する体験の信頼度を上げる話。Babylon 9.28.0のalpha depth pre-passとroot motionは、透明素材と移動の履歴を同じ深度面に載せられる。CodropsのWide gridは、横長レイアウトそのものをshader化する良い入口で、9/29のLOD / parallaxと接続すると、gridが単なるUIではなく品質・奥行き・負荷の譜面になる。

The PlayCanvas fix for material-less mesh acceptance is quiet but useful: production scenes often contain placed objects whose material has not arrived yet. The v2.22.5 gsplat depth-picking hang avoidance is also about the reliability of editing, selecting, and measuring splats, not just stability. Babylon 9.28.0's alpha depth pre-pass and root motion let transparent material and movement history share the same depth surface. Codrops' Wide grid is a strong entry point for turning a horizontal layout itself into shader material; connected to September 29's LOD / parallax work, the grid becomes a score for quality, depth, and load.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

renderer / layer受付系の最小schemaは `frameId`, `meshInstanceId`, `layerId`, `materialReady`, `layerAccepted`, `fallbackMaterialUsed`, `screenParentId`, `elementPositionFixed`。splat picking系は `splatId`, `depthPickRisk`, `gpuVendorRisk`, `pickRadius`, `lastSafePickFrame`, `hangAvoided`。depth / alpha / motion系は `alphaCutOff`, `depthPrepassEnabled`, `rootMotionDelta`, `dataTextureSampling`, `wgslPluginState`。three.js移行系は `pmremTargetKind`, `weakRefTracked`, `finalizedAtFrame`, `xrSourceEye`, `viewportScaleApplied`, `scissorScaleApplied`, `pixelSizeNumber`。9/29の `textureVersion`, `residentBytes`, `mipLevel` と合わせると、素材到着、受付、選択、深度、破棄の一連が1枚のstate atlasになる。

A minimal renderer / layer acceptance schema is `frameId`, `meshInstanceId`, `layerId`, `materialReady`, `layerAccepted`, `fallbackMaterialUsed`, `screenParentId`, and `elementPositionFixed`. For splat picking, use `splatId`, `depthPickRisk`, `gpuVendorRisk`, `pickRadius`, `lastSafePickFrame`, and `hangAvoided`. For depth / alpha / motion, use `alphaCutOff`, `depthPrepassEnabled`, `rootMotionDelta`, `dataTextureSampling`, and `wgslPluginState`. For three.js migration, use `pmremTargetKind`, `weakRefTracked`, `finalizedAtFrame`, `xrSourceEye`, `viewportScaleApplied`, `scissorScaleApplied`, and `pixelSizeNumber`. Combined with September 29's `textureVersion`, `residentBytes`, and `mipLevel`, material arrival, acceptance, selection, depth, and disposal become one state atlas.
