Connect splat light, shadow, and inspection surfaces into a renderer state score

今日は、three.js r187に向けたWebGPU/TSL/renderer境界の整理、PlayCanvas v2.23.0のGSplat LOD/nearest-first streaming、Babylon.js 9.29.0のGaussian splats対応frame graphとocclusion/texture周辺、そしてVLAM!のthree.js WebGPU Gaussian Splat viewerを軸に見た。10/8のpre-pass / compile heat / splat residencyから一段進め、splat空間の光・影・LOD・観測UIを同じ状態譜面として扱う方向に整理した。本命サンプルはvolumetric light / streamed splat / proxy shadow、派生サンプルはframe graph / occlusion visibility / texture contractの観測面。

Today's report focuses on three.js r187 WebGPU/TSL/renderer boundary work, PlayCanvas v2.23.0 GSplat LOD and nearest-first streaming, Babylon.js 9.29.0 frame graph support for Gaussian splats plus occlusion/texture work, and VLAM!, a three.js WebGPU Gaussian Splat viewer. Extending 10/8's pre-pass, compile heat, and splat residency thread, the report treats splat-space light, shadow, LOD, and inspection UI as one renderer state score. The main sample explores volumetric light, streamed splats, and proxy shadows; the derived sample stages frame graph lanes, occlusion visibility, and texture contracts.

注目事例と何が面白いかNotable examples and why they matter

今日いちばん面白いのはVLAM!の位置づけ。three.jsのWebGPU rendererに乗り、WebGL2 fallbackも持つsplat viewerが、streamed splat、proxy relighting、depth of field、warpingのような演出側の語彙へつながっている。PlayCanvasはすでにGSplat LODとstreamingをengine releaseの中心に置き、Babylonはframe graphへsplatsを入れている。つまり『splatを描く』段階から、『splatがどのLODで、どのpassに入り、どのocclusion/texture契約で見えるか』を演出に使う段階へ進んでいる。CodropsのWide grid、cursor trail、datamosh、face maskのような事例は、この観測面をWeb表現として見せる際のテンポやUI密度の参照になる。

The most interesting item today is VLAM!'s position. A splat viewer built on the three.js WebGPU renderer with WebGL2 fallback connects streamed splats, proxy relighting, depth of field, and warping to expressive vocabulary. PlayCanvas already places GSplat LOD and streaming at the center of engine releases, while Babylon brings splats into the frame graph. This means the field is moving from simply drawing splats to using which LOD, pass, occlusion state, and texture contract makes a splat visible as an expressive layer. Codrops examples such as wide grids, cursor trails, datamosh, and face masks are useful references for the tempo and UI density of exposing those inspection surfaces as web expression.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

ログ化候補は `splatId/lodLevel/streamRank/splatBudget/distanceSq/proxyMeshId/relightEnabled/beamEnergy/shadowReceiverTile/frameGraphPassId/occlusionVisible/occlusionQueryAge/textureContractId/textureReady/retryCount/pipelineCompileMs/cacheHit/viewportScale/xrEye`。実装は、実viewerのログを低解像度state textureへ入れ、fragment shaderで点、光条、影タイル、pass laneへ変換するのが軽い。PlayCanvas側ならGSplat LODとAppStats、three.js/VLAM側ならSplatMesh updateとrenderer compile timings、Babylon側ならframe graph passとocclusion visibilityが入り口になる。

Candidate log fields include `splatId/lodLevel/streamRank/splatBudget/distanceSq/proxyMeshId/relightEnabled/beamEnergy/shadowReceiverTile/frameGraphPassId/occlusionVisible/occlusionQueryAge/textureContractId/textureReady/retryCount/pipelineCompileMs/cacheHit/viewportScale/xrEye`. A lightweight implementation path is to pack real viewer logs into a low-resolution state texture, then convert them in a fragment shader into points, light beams, shadow tiles, and pass lanes. For PlayCanvas, GSplat LOD and AppStats are entry points. For three.js/VLAM, SplatMesh updates and renderer compile timings are useful. For Babylon, frame graph passes and occlusion visibility are the right hooks.

俯瞰メモBird's-eye memo

最近の傾向は、描画品質の制御が『裏側の最適化』から『演出の入力』へ変わっていること。9月後半はresource cleanup、readback、sort precision、mipmap/residency、layer/depthを扱い、10月はviewport/scissor、view bind group、Inspector/AppStats、variant、HTML/particle/cursor、pre-pass/compileへ広がった。今日のsplat light/shadowは、その流れを空間演出へ戻す橋になる。組み合わせ候補は、compile heatを光の脈、streamRankを点群の呼吸、occlusion visibilityを見える/見えないの拍、texture retryをちらつく字幕にする構成。育てる価値が高いテーマは、renderer内部の安全策や性能策を、鑑賞者が感じる空間の気配として扱うこと。

The recent trend is that rendering quality control is shifting from hidden optimization to expressive input. Late September covered resource cleanup, readback, sort precision, mipmap/residency, and layer/depth; October expanded into viewport/scissor, view bind groups, Inspector/AppStats, variants, HTML/particle/cursor, and pre-pass/compile. Today's splat light/shadow thread is a bridge back into spatial staging. Strong combinations include compile heat as light pulse, streamRank as point-cloud breathing, occlusion visibility as visible/invisible beats, and texture retry as flickering subtitles. The high-value theme is treating renderer safety and performance mechanisms as spatial atmosphere felt by the viewer.

サンプルデータの可能性と組み合わせ考察Possibilities for the sample data and combinations

今回の2本は `probeX/probeY/mode/pulse` に加え、`splatBudget/streamRank/lodLevel/beamEnergy/proxyShadow/occlusionVisible/textureReady/retryCount/passId` を時系列データとして扱える。音反応ならkickをstream pulse、snareをproxy shadowの反転、hi-hatをtexture retry tile、bassをvolumetric beamの太さへ割り当てる。MediaPipeなら手の距離でsplatBudget、手首速度でbeamEnergy、顔の向きでocclusion focusを変える。WebXRではcontroller rayで受光タイルを選び、視線の滞在時間でLODが上がる展示にできる。ブランド演出なら、商品や空間のsplatが近づくほど解像し、読み込み中の状態そのものが光の呼吸として見える。

The two samples can treat `probeX/probeY/mode/pulse` plus `splatBudget/streamRank/lodLevel/beamEnergy/proxyShadow/occlusionVisible/textureReady/retryCount/passId` as time-series data. For audio, map kick to stream pulse, snare to proxy-shadow inversion, hi-hat to texture retry tiles, and bass to volumetric beam width. With MediaPipe, hand distance can drive splatBudget, wrist velocity beamEnergy, and face direction occlusion focus. In WebXR, controller rays could select receiver tiles while gaze dwell increases LOD. For branded experiences, a product or place captured as splats could resolve as the viewer approaches, with the loading state itself visible as breathing light.
