制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

incremental bakeを実装へ進めるなら、probeIndex、passIndex、bounceIndex、sunCascadeIndex、durationMs、dirtyReasonをJSON logにして、1秒ごとのAppStats / MiniStats値と同じtimeline textureへ入れるのがよい。ベイク済みprobeをただ点灯するだけでなく、未完了は冷色、pass中は縞、前pass参照中は低周波の揺れ、と状態を分けると進捗が表現になる。bilinear tap merge側は、raw tap count、merged tap count、sigma、mip level、threshold、GPU durationを分けて保持する。tap削減分を全部品質向上に使うのではなく、一部をinteractionや音反応の帯域へ割ると、性能最適化が見た目の個性になる。

To take incremental baking further, log probeIndex, passIndex, bounceIndex, sunCascadeIndex, durationMs, and dirtyReason as JSON, then feed them into the same timeline texture as AppStats / MiniStats values. Rather than lighting completed probes only, use cool colors for pending cells, stripes for in-pass cells, and low-frequency motion for previous-pass sampling. For bilinear tap merge, keep raw tap count, merged tap count, sigma, mip level, threshold, and GPU duration as separate channels. Do not spend all saved taps on higher quality; reserve some for interaction or audio bands so optimization becomes a visual signature.

サンプルデータの可能性と組み合わせ考察Possibilities for the sample data and combinations

今日の2サンプルは、入力値をデータとして保存しやすい。1本目はcell id、bake order、done state、pass、sun vector、pulseを持てる。2本目はraw tap response、merged response、mip energy、threshold、tap offsetを持てる。組み合わせると、音の低域でprobe burst、中域でbake cursor、高域でtap offsetを動かし、WebXRでは視線が当たったprobeだけ次passへ進める演出にできる。画像入力を使う場合は、明るい領域だけprobe完了扱いにして、暗部はbilinear bloomで遅れて浮かせる。ブランド演出なら、ロゴを完成形として出すのではなく、ベイク完了・tap budget・負荷履歴が積み上がった結果として現れる設計が合う。

Both samples can be logged as data. The first can store cell id, bake order, done state, pass, sun vector, and pulse. The second can store raw tap response, merged response, mip energy, threshold, and tap offset. Combined with audio, low bands can trigger probe bursts, mids can move the bake cursor, and highs can shift tap offset. In WebXR, only gaze-hit probes could advance to the next pass. With image input, bright regions can count as completed probes while dark regions bloom in later. For brand experiences, the logo should emerge as the result of bake completion, tap budget, and performance history rather than appearing as a finished asset.
