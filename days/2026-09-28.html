制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

A minimal mobile-postprocess diagnostic schema is `frameId`, `frameMs`, `refreshHz`, `devicePixelRatio`, `physicalPixels`, `renderTargetPixels`, `passCount`, `sampleCount`, `usesOutputPass`, `usesGammaCorrectionShader`, `composerEnabled`, and `droppedFrameCount`. For WebGPURenderer UBO pressure, use `renderItemCount`, `meshCount`, `instancedCount`, `uniformUploadBytes`, `bindGroupCount`, `pipelineChanges`, and `materialChanges`. For Babylon / water, use `alphaCutOff`, `depthPrepassEnabled`, `rootMotionDelta`, `splatTextureFilter`, `vPartIndexPluginCount`, `objTextureWaitMs`, and `causticEnergy`.
