制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

ログ化候補は `gbufferTileId/prepassEnabled/passKind/compileAsyncBatch/pipelineCompileMs/nodeIntentId/nodeBuildShared/cacheHit/storageBufferArrayBind/dispatchSizeValid/gsplatBudget/gsplatLodLevel/nearestStreamRank/stochasticAlphaSeed/gaussianPointCount/inspectorLayerId/residencyAge/gpuParticleTextureReady`。実装ではまずJSONログを1フレームごとの低解像度state textureに変換し、fragment shader側でtile、lane、ring、splatへ変える。WebGPU本体を直接書かなくても、WebGL shaderだけで『WebGPU/rendererの状態が舞台化される』見た目を短時間で検証できる。

Candidate log fields include `gbufferTileId/prepassEnabled/passKind/compileAsyncBatch/pipelineCompileMs/nodeIntentId/nodeBuildShared/cacheHit/storageBufferArrayBind/dispatchSizeValid/gsplatBudget/gsplatLodLevel/nearestStreamRank/stochasticAlphaSeed/gaussianPointCount/inspectorLayerId/residencyAge/gpuParticleTextureReady`. For implementation, first convert JSON logs into a low-resolution state texture per frame, then have the fragment shader turn that into tiles, lanes, rings, and splats. Even without writing full WebGPU, a WebGL shader can quickly test the look of WebGPU/renderer state being staged.
