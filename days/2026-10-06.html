注目事例と何が面白いかNotable examples and why they matter

今日の中心は、見えないdepth / history / buffer layoutを表現の主役にすること。depth-only targetは最終色を持たないが、shadowやprepassのための『見えない舞台』として非常に強い。TemporalReprojectNodeのhistory depth typeは、過去フレームが現在フレームへ戻ってくる条件そのものを演出にできる。struct storage buffer array bindは地味だが、GPU上の構造体列を安定したlaneとして読めるので、10/5のprogram variant cacheと同じく『内部状態の譜面化』に向いている。PlayCanvasのGSplat sort flickerはbugとしては直したいが、同一depth keyが揺れる現象は、意図的な粒子のざわめきや不安定な記憶表現にも転用できる。

Today's center of gravity is making invisible depth, history, and buffer layout the main expressive material. A depth-only target has no final color, but as an invisible stage for shadows and prepasses it is powerful. TemporalReprojectNode history depth typing lets the acceptance conditions for past frames become part of the performance. Struct storage buffer array binding is subtle, but it turns GPU struct rows into stable lanes, fitting the same internal-state-score direction as 10/5's program variant cache. PlayCanvas GSplat sort flicker should be fixed as a bug, yet the equal-depth-key instability can also be repurposed as particle shimmer or unstable memory.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

ログ化候補はthree.js側が `depthOnlyTargetId/colorAttachmentCount/msaaSamples/historyDepthType/reprojectAccepted/storageStructLength/storageArrayMode/nodeBuildShared`、PlayCanvas側が `gsplatDepthKey/equalDepthGroup/sortFrameHash/flickerCount/cascadeIndex/cascadeStorageMode/atlasCell/textureArrayLayer`、Babylon側が `gaussianPointId/pointBudget/radius/nearestSampling/inspectorLitePanel/fluidDiffuseReady/materialExtension`。実装では、depth-only passやhistory rejectをそのままdebug UIに出すより、8x8から16x16のstate textureにして、shader側でlaneや粒子密度へ変換するほうが作品化しやすい。

Candidate log fields are `depthOnlyTargetId/colorAttachmentCount/msaaSamples/historyDepthType/reprojectAccepted/storageStructLength/storageArrayMode/nodeBuildShared` for three.js, `gsplatDepthKey/equalDepthGroup/sortFrameHash/flickerCount/cascadeIndex/cascadeStorageMode/atlasCell/textureArrayLayer` for PlayCanvas, and `gaussianPointId/pointBudget/radius/nearestSampling/inspectorLitePanel/fluidDiffuseReady/materialExtension` for Babylon. For implementation, instead of exposing depth-only passes or history rejection as debug UI, encode them into an 8x8 to 16x16 state texture and let shaders convert that into lanes and particle density.

サンプルデータの可能性と組み合わせ考察Possibilities for the sample data and combinations

今回の2本は、入力を `probeX/probeY/mode/pulse/viewerPressure/cascadeProbe`、状態を `depthOnlyTargetId/colorAttachmentCount/historyAccepted/historyAge/storageStructLength/arrayLane/equalDepthGroup/sortHash/cascadeLayer/gaussianRadius/pointBudget` として保存できる。音反応ではkickでhistory reject、snareでdepth resolve、hi-hatでstorage lane scan、bassでGaussian budgetを動かす。WebXRでは左右eyeごとにhistory acceptanceを分け、controller速度をsortHashの安定/不安定へ流す。展示では、観客の距離でdepth-only passだけが濃くなり、近づくほどsplat sortが安定し、離れるほど記憶がちらつく構成が強い。

The two samples can store inputs as `probeX/probeY/mode/pulse/viewerPressure/cascadeProbe` and state as `depthOnlyTargetId/colorAttachmentCount/historyAccepted/historyAge/storageStructLength/arrayLane/equalDepthGroup/sortHash/cascadeLayer/gaussianRadius/pointBudget`. For audio, kick can reject history, snare can trigger depth resolve, hi-hats can scan storage lanes, and bass can drive Gaussian budget. For WebXR, split history acceptance per eye and feed controller velocity into sortHash stability. In an installation, visitor distance could thicken only the depth-only pass, stabilize splat sorting up close, and make memory flicker at distance.
