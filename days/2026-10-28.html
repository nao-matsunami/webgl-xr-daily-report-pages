制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

共通state scoreへ `probeGridId`, `influenceBounds`, `visibilityWeight`, `occluderDepth`, `drawerMode`, `visibleInstanceCount`, `culledInstanceCount`, `lodLevel`, `streamRank`, `fogEnabled`, `toneMapMode`, `passId`, `passMs`, `gpuFrameMs`, `captureId` を記録する。DDGIはprobeの有効範囲とleak量を別表示にし、visibility bufferは『消えた』instanceも数える。WebGL fallbackでは同じ状態をCPU側のinstance filterとCanvas/WebGL point spriteで再現し、GPU専用の表現に固定しない。

Log probe bounds, visibility, culling, arrival and GPU timing state. Keep visibility loss measurable and provide a WebGL fallback with CPU filtering.

サンプルデータの可能性と組み合わせ考察Possibilities for sample data and combinations

Visibility, culling, bounds, paths, captures and GPU time form a log of what was seen and retained. Combine with audio, camera, face pose and XR gaze for live or material-led experiences.
