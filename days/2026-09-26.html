注目事例と何が面白いかNotable examples and why they matter

three.jsのTSL compute / FFT2D方向は、音響・海面・粒子・post effectだけでなく、rendererの状態ログを周波数化する発想に向く。例えばpipeline compile時間、WGSL size、sort flicker、readback latencyを周波数帯に分ければ、技術診断がそのままVJ的な入力になる。Codropsのcustom-shaped cursor trailは、pointerを単なるUI補助ではなく、mask texture / brush stamp / decay bufferとして使う例として重要。Babylonのalpha / RootMotion更新と組み合わせると、material arrivalやmotion deltaをcursor brushでなぞって点検する表現が作れる。

The three.js TSL compute / FFT2D direction is useful not only for audio, ocean surfaces, particles, and post effects, but also for frequency-mapping renderer state logs. Pipeline compile time, WGSL size, sort flicker, and readback latency can become frequency bands, turning technical diagnostics into VJ-like input. Codrops' custom-shaped cursor trail matters because the pointer becomes a mask texture, brush stamp, and decay buffer instead of a simple UI helper. Combined with Babylon alpha / RootMotion updates, material arrival and motion deltas can be inspected by brushing across them.
