表現・インタラクションのアイデアExpression and interaction ideas

本命案は「Uniform Query Visibility Scope」。uniform struct array、RENDER / FRAME group update、normalized occlusion query、CSM shadow branch、background blur tapsを1枚の監査面にして、dragでvisibility probe、clickでuniform refresh pulseを走らせる。派生案は「Framegraph Splat Geometry Pass」。frame graph lanesの中にsplat / sprite / particle geometry pass、ImageBitmap upload path、streaming compound compact、fixed horizontal FOV correctionを重ね、dragでcamera gate、clickでcompact pulseを動かす。どちらも「結果の画」ではなく、描画を成立させる内部状態を楽器化する方向。

The main idea is Uniform Query Visibility Scope: place uniform struct arrays, RENDER / FRAME group updates, normalized occlusion queries, CSM shadow branches, and background-blur taps on one inspection surface. Drag moves the visibility probe; click triggers a uniform-refresh pulse. The derived idea is Framegraph Splat Geometry Pass: layer splat / sprite / particle geometry passes, ImageBitmap upload paths, streaming-compound compaction, and fixed horizontal-FOV correction into frame-graph lanes. Drag moves the camera gate; click emits a compact pulse. Both turn the internal state that makes rendering possible into an instrument rather than a final picture.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

uniform系は `groupId`, `frameId`, `renderId`, `structIndex`, `dirtyBit`, `uploadBytes`, `lastUsedNode`, `visibilityWeight` を小さなtexture laneに落とすと、three.js TSL / WebGPUとBabylon ProceduralTexture uniform setterの両方に対応しやすい。occlusion queryは `visibleSamples / totalSamples` を正規化し、0.0から1.0の連続値でshaderへ戻すと、HUD、scanline、sound reactive、splat densityに使える。frame graph系は `passName`, `resourceId`, `geometryKind`, `splatCount`, `spriteCount`, `particleCount`, `imageBitmapFastPath`, `compactGeneration`, `fovMode` を持つと、Babylon frame graphとPlayCanvas / SuperSplatのstream状態を同じビューに置ける。

For uniform work, map `groupId`, `frameId`, `renderId`, `structIndex`, `dirtyBit`, `uploadBytes`, `lastUsedNode`, and `visibilityWeight` into small texture lanes. This can bridge three.js TSL / WebGPU and Babylon ProceduralTexture uniform setters. For occlusion queries, normalize `visibleSamples / totalSamples` and feed the continuous 0.0 to 1.0 value back into shaders for HUDs, scanlines, sound response, or splat density. For frame graphs, track `passName`, `resourceId`, `geometryKind`, `splatCount`, `spriteCount`, `particleCount`, `imageBitmapFastPath`, `compactGeneration`, and `fovMode` so Babylon frame graph state and PlayCanvas / SuperSplat streaming state can share one view.
