Weave invisible draws and light-leak control into spatial afterglow

This report connects three.js LightProbeGrid DDGI visibility weighting, SH-atlas compute updates and GPU-driven drawers with PlayCanvas view uniforms and distance-prioritized GSplat LOD, plus Babylon Gaussian Splat rendering, through two interactive Canvas studies.

PlayCanvas v2.23系はview uniformと距離優先・coarsest-firstなGSplat LODを整え、注視領域に品質を集める設計を後押しする。5. Babylon.jsのGaussian Splatデータテクスチャnearest samplingは、点群の状態を意図せずにじませず読ませる土台になる。

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

Log probe, visibility, SH, drawer, view and LOD metrics in one score. Design probe influence bounds and give candidate, visible and deferred states distinct artistic roles.
