制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction note

再投影のhistoryは前frameをそのままmixせず、velocityでUVを戻し、depth差・normal差・画面外でconfidenceを落とす。動体はobject motion vectorを足さないと背景だけ正しくても輪郭が滲む。FBOはRGBA16Fとlinear filterを使えるか確認し、不可ならRGBA8＋ditherへfallbackする。bundle/textureはdispose後の参照を持つ設計を避け、generation IDまたはnull guardで再作成を明示する。PlayCanvas computeはWebGPU限定なのでWebGL2 fallbackを分ける。

Do not simply mix the prior frame: reproject UVs with velocity and reduce confidence for depth/normal mismatch or out-of-screen samples. Moving objects need object motion vectors. Check RGBA16F+linear filtering and fall back to dithered RGBA8; avoid references to disposed textures with generation IDs or null guards. PlayCanvas compute is WebGPU-only, so keep a WebGL2 fallback.

サンプルデータの可能性と組み合わせ考察Possibilities for the sample data and combinations

Pointer coordinates/speed, seed times, FBO confidence and lantern generation/lifetime form participation time-series data. Combine them with audio envelopes, camera stillness, MediaPipe hand speed, XR gaze dwell and venue density: trusted history can ring lower and longer while expired lanterns revive through another visitor. Persist one score into URL state and offline renders to connect web, film and installation.
