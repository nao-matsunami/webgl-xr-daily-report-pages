Gaussian Splat Lite treats depth rendering and streaming LOD inside Three.js, making splats production inputs that can be composited with normal 3D rather than just background captures. September Codrops examples such as datamosh and shape-aware ASCII make the breaking of resolution itself the subject. Today's samples treat mipmaps and LOD not just as optimization, but as expressive evidence of which resolution is resident on the GPU right now.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

texture / mipmap系の最小schemaは `frameId`, `textureId`, `textureVersion`, `mipLevel`, `mipmapsAutoUpdate`, `uploadedBytes`, `residentBytes`, `cacheHit`, `framebufferSourceCopied`, `colorSpace`, `disposeQueued`。TSL / WebGPU側は `nodeCount`, `cachedConversionCount`, `scalarConstCacheHit`, `gatherSampleCount`, `bindGroupCount`, `pipelineCompileMs`。splat / parallax / depth側は `lodLevel`, `lodError`, `splatBudget`, `streamPacketBytes`, `parallaxSteps`, `shadowCascade`, `alphaCutOff`, `rootMotionDelta`, `depthPrepassEnabled`。9/28の `frameMs`, `passCount`, `uniformUploadBytes` と合わせると、重さ、解像度、滞在、深度の4軸が同じtexture atlasに載る。

A minimal texture / mipmap schema is `frameId`, `textureId`, `textureVersion`, `mipLevel`, `mipmapsAutoUpdate`, `uploadedBytes`, `residentBytes`, `cacheHit`, `framebufferSourceCopied`, `colorSpace`, and `disposeQueued`. For TSL / WebGPU, use `nodeCount`, `cachedConversionCount`, `scalarConstCacheHit`, `gatherSampleCount`, `bindGroupCount`, and `pipelineCompileMs`. For splat / parallax / depth, use `lodLevel`, `lodError`, `splatBudget`, `streamPacketBytes`, `parallaxSteps`, `shadowCascade`, `alphaCutOff`, `rootMotionDelta`, and `depthPrepassEnabled`. Combined with September 28's `frameMs`, `passCount`, and `uniformUploadBytes`, heaviness, resolution, residency, and depth can share one texture atlas.
