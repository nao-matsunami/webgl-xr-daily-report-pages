This report connects three.js draw ranges, static-bundle disposal and async shadow compilation with PlayCanvas coarse-first GSplat LOD and Babylon Gaussian Splat texture sampling, adding two dependency-free Canvas studies for performable arrival, rebuild and shadow states.
