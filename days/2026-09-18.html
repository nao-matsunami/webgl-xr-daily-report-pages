制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction notes

splat relight側は、coverage、depth、bakedLighting、relightVector、hullShadowMask、hudTexture、scanlineMask、workerLoadingPhaseを小さなstate textureに分けると実装しやすい。shadow casterは厳密meshでなくても、splat中心点の2D convex hullや低解像depth silhouetteから始められる。stream側は、chunkId、lodLevel、requestedBytes、decodedBytes、gpuUploadedBytes、sortTicket、sortAgeMs、lastValidOrder、fadeWeight、stochasticActiveを持つと、GSLやPlayCanvas SOG系の状態を共通表示に落としやすい。9/17のbrushPathはselectionFootprintとしてdepth passに戻し、9/16のHTMLTexture dirty rectはHUD texture更新のコスト表示に戻す。

For splat relighting, split coverage, depth, bakedLighting, relightVector, hullShadowMask, hudTexture, scanlineMask, and workerLoadingPhase into small state textures. The shadow caster can begin as a 2D convex hull over splat centers or a low-resolution depth silhouette rather than a strict mesh. For streaming, track chunkId, lodLevel, requestedBytes, decodedBytes, gpuUploadedBytes, sortTicket, sortAgeMs, lastValidOrder, fadeWeight, and stochasticActive to map GSL or PlayCanvas SOG state into a common display. Feed September 17's brushPath back as a selectionFootprint in the depth pass, and September 16's HTMLTexture dirty rectangles back into HUD texture upload cost.
